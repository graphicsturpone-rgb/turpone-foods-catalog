ingredients:
  - 2 teaspoons minced shallot
  - 1/4 cup aged balsamic vinegar
  - 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
  - 1 tablespoon <strong><a href="https://martha.com/" target="_blank">Martha Stewart's</a></strong> Fig Oregano Honey vinegar
  - 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  - Pinch of dried oregano
  - Sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
  - 1/2 cup extra virgin olive oil
instructions:
  - "In a medium bowl (or a clean mason jar), combine the minced shallot and vinegars. Let stand 10 minutes to mellow flavor of shallot."
  - "Whisk in Martha Stewart’s Fig Oregano Honey, Dijon mustard and dried oregano. While whisking constantly with one hand, slowly pour the extra virgin olive oil into the bowl in a thin, steady stream until thick and fully incorporated. (If using a mason jar, secure the lid tightly and shake vigorously for 30 seconds until uniform.) Season with salt and pepper."
nutrition: ''

An elegant, Mediterranean-inspired dressing that marries deep, jammy fig blossom notes with aromatic oregano. This vinaigrette relies on proper emulsification to create a smooth, unified coating for crisp salad greens. This dressing is a lovely complement to a salad of bitter greens (like arugula and radicchios) served with warmed goat cheese.
