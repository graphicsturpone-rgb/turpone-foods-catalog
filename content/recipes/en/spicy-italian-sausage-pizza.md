---
title: Spicy Italian Sausage Pizza
image: /assets/images/recipes/Spicy Italian Sausage Pizza - Edited.jpg
prepTime: 20 minutes (plus dough preparation)
cookTime: 45 minutes
calories: 320 kcal
yields: 1 pizza (4 servings)
upsellImage: ''
upsellLink: ''
ingredients:
  - 1 package <strong><a href="https://martha.com/" target="_blank">Martha Stewart's</a></strong> Neapolitan Style Pizza Dough Mix, prepared per package directions
  - 1/2 cup <strong><a href="https://martha.com/" target="_blank">Martha Stewart's</a></strong> Spicy Pizza Sauce
  - 2 tablespoons <strong><a href="https://martha.com/" target="_blank">Martha Stewart's</a></strong> Spicy Garlic Infused Extra-Virgin Olive Oil, plus more for drizzling
  - 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  - 8 ounces Italian sausage, cooked and crumbled
  - 1/2 bell pepper, thinly sliced (red, yellow, orange or a mix)
  - 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  - 1 tablespoon <strong><a href="https://martha.com/" target="_blank">Martha Stewart's</a></strong> Triple Pepper Mix Seasoning
instructions:
  - "Prepare the Neapolitan Style Pizza Dough Mix according to package directions and stretch into a 10-inch round."
  - "Heat the oven and pizza stone to 500°F (260°C)."
  - "Brush the dough lightly with the Spicy Garlic Olive Oil, then spread the Spicy Pizza Sauce evenly across the surface."
  - "Sprinkle the mozzarella over the sauce. Evenly distribute the sausage, peppers, and onions. Finish with the Triple Pepper Mix Seasoning."
  - "Bake 8 to 10 minutes, until the crust is puffed with light charring and the cheese has melted completely."
  - "Drizzle a small amount of Spicy Garlic Olive Oil over the hot pizza before slicing."
nutrition: ''
---

A bold pizza layered with spicy tomato sauce, Italian sausage, and colorful peppers, with Triple Pepper Mix Seasoning and infused olive oil bringing out the natural heat and savory profile.
