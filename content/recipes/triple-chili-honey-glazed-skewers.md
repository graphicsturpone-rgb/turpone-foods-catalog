ingredients:
  - 1.5 lbs chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch cubes
  - 3 tbsp Triple Chili Honey Drizzle
  - 2 tbsp soy sauce
  - 1 tbsp olive oil
  - 3 cloves garlic, minced
  - 1 red onion, cut into 1-inch chunks
instructions:
  - 'Marinate the Protein: In a medium bowl, whisk together 1 tablespoon of the Triple Chili Honey Drizzle, soy sauce, olive oil, and garlic.'
  - 'Thread the Skewers: Alternate chicken and onion chunks on skewers.'
  - 'Grill: Preheat grill to medium-high heat. Grill skewers for 10-12 minutes, turning occasionally.'
  - 'Glaze: During the last 2 minutes, brush the remaining Triple Chili Honey Drizzle over the skewers.'

This sweet and spicy glaze pairs beautifully with smoky, flame-kissed proteins. The honey is utilized two ways: first to build flavor in a quick marinade, then applied at the very end of cooking to create a thick, glossy, caramelized finish.
